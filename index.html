IRONDESK GYM APP

Goodbye Paper Register. Hello Control.

IronDesk is a gym management app that solves the three biggest problems of the paper register: fee tracking, membership renewals, and absent members. It has 4 roles (Member, Reception, Trainer, Owner) and 11 screens, with real-time data sync on Firebase. It works as a PWA and also installs as an Android APK.

  • Member: online join request, personal QR code, workout plan, and trainer-assigned diet plan
  • Reception: QR-scan attendance, fee entry with partial payments, renewal list, and join-request approval
  • Trainer: add or remove own members and assign diet plans
  • Owner: daily and monthly collection reports, pending-fee list, absent members (7+ days), and staff management
  • Reminders: one-tap WhatsApp reminders for fees and renewals
  • Security: Firebase Authentication with role-based Firestore security rules
